How to use
1. Pick a format (Reel/Story is best for Instagram)
2. Add photos and/or short clips, set seconds per slide, reorder with ↑ ↓
3. Add an optional caption per slide
4. Upload your own music/voiceover — never use audio you don't have rights to; original sound or royalty-free tracks only
5. Preview, then Export → downloads a video file ready to upload

Reels perform best under 30s — the duration badge above turns amber past 30s and red past 60s.
